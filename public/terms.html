Terms of use

Finance Tracker is a personal, private, non-commercial project for the owner of authorized accounts. It is not affiliated with any bank.

Banking tools are read-only. They cannot make payments, create transfers or provide unrestricted access to the filesystem.

Results depend on bank availability, history limits and synchronization freshness. Categories, recurring payments, alerts and forecasts are estimates to review.

Amounts remain separate by currency. Analyses are not financial, tax or legal advice. Check decisions against original bank records.

The owner can stop the service and revoke consent. Revocation does not automatically delete stored information.
