Privacy

This personal project processes account metadata, balances, dates, amounts, currencies, counterparties and transaction descriptions to help the owner understand their finances.

Bank access

Enable Banking provides read-only PSD2 access after bank authorization. Consent is time-limited and can be revoked through the bank. The application does not receive your banking password.

Storage and security

The original installation uses a personal Netcup VPS. Local session files have restrictive permissions. Version 2 stores financial payloads in an encrypted SQLite database outside the repository, with a separate key. These protections require correct configuration and deployment; they do not protect against a compromised server that can access the key.

Data shared with OpenAI

When an MCP tool is called, information needed for the response — which may include amounts, merchants and descriptions — is sent to OpenAI through the private tunnel and used in ChatGPT. Your OpenAI account’s privacy and retention settings apply separately. Version 2 does not expose keys, JWTs, sessions or full IBANs by default.

Retention and deletion

History remains on the server until the owner deletes it. No automatic purge is configured. The same applies to backups and local reports. Revoking bank consent stops bank access but does not erase local copies or OpenAI conversations.

Scope and contact

This is a personal, non-commercial installation for accounts whose owner has authorized access. Applicable legal and data protection requirements must be reassessed before offering it to other people. No personal contact address is published here.

Your private server address

The private server entry page saves only your server’s HTTPS origin in this browser’s local storage, after you explicitly confirm. It never saves passwords, tokens, bank codes or financial data. Use “Forget this address” to remove the saved origin. The page makes no requests to your server before you choose to open it; GitHub Pages does not host your private banking service or centrally store this address.

Appearance preference

The theme button saves only your light or dark appearance preference in this browser’s local storage. Until you choose, the site follows your system appearance. No preference is sent to a server.

This website uses no advertising, analytics or application cookies. The site host and bank provider may process technical logs, including the initial callback URL. A static page cannot prevent that logging.
